2ndPressing Declicker

Click and pop removal for vinyl: a compact neural network finds the clicks, the music on either side rebuilds them, and everything else passes through bit for bit.

This installer can add:

  • 2ndPressing app: play a turntable through any audio interface and hear it cleaned live (in Applications).
  • Audio Unit: for Logic Pro, GarageBand and other AU hosts.
  • VST3 and CLAP plugins: for other DAWs.

Choose Customize on the Installation Type step to pick only some of them.
