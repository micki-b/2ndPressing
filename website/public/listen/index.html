Each demo opens a working replica of the plugin. Press play, then switch with its own keys: REPAIRED for the declicked audio, BYPASS for the original, CLICKS to hear only what was removed. All three play in sync.

A note on these examples. 2ndPressing’s results are much more noticeable on modern recordings. Copyright limits what can be published here, so these demos use 78 rpm records made before 1926, which are in the public domain.

About these recordings

Both are unrestored transfers from the Internet Archive’s Great 78 Project, so the crackle is the record’s own. US recordings published before 1926 and compositions published before 1931 are in the public domain.

Each clip is 30 seconds at 48 kHz, processed by the real engine at sensitivity 50 % with percussion protection Off. 78s crackle far more than a typical LP, so the meter sits near the top of its scale.
